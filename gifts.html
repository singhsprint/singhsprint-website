Questions

Will my clients actually wear something with my logo on it?

Only if the logo is small. That's why our default is a tonal left-chest or sleeve mark, and you can go with no logo on the gear at all - your brand on the box and the card does the job. Big logos are for your own team.

What if a client doesn't pick a size?

We remind them on day 2 and day 5. After 14 days we go with your default size (L unless you say otherwise), and unclaimed boxes can ship to you in bulk. Nothing is embroidered or printed before the size is in.

How long does it take?

Mockups within 1 business day. After you approve: about 2 weeks for a printed tee + cap box, longer for embroidered pieces - we give you the date before you pay. Holiday orders: book in September or October.

Can I hand them out myself?

Yes. Choose "Deliver to me" and we send the inner boxes to you in bulk, no outer shipper, lower price.

Do I pay everything up front?

A deposit at checkout, the balance when the boxes ship. Nothing is made until you approve the mockups.

Is this tax deductible?

Branded apparel is generally a fully deductible promotional expense, unlike food and drink gifts which fall under the 50% rule. We itemize the invoice so your accountant can see what's what. Confirm with them.

Ready to send something they'll keep?

Two minutes to tell us about the box. Mockups and a price within 1 business day.
