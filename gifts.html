How it works

Four steps. No size spreadsheet.

The hard part of gifting apparel is sizes. We took it off your plate.

Pick the pieces

Start from a tier or a budget. Tees, crewnecks, quarter-zips, caps - the same catalog as our shop.

Add your logo

Upload it once. We keep it small and tonal by default, because that’s what gets worn.

Paste your client list

Name and email is enough. Each client gets a private link to pick a size and confirm an address.

We pack and ship

Mockups first - nothing is made until you approve. Then each box ships with its own tracking.

The size problem, solved

Your clients pick their own size.

Every client gets a short, private link - in English or French. They pick a size for each piece and confirm where to ship it. You watch it fill in from your order page.

  • Reminders on day 2 and day 5, so you don’t have to chase anyone
  • No answer after 14 days? We ship your default size
  • English or French, whichever they read
  • Addresses stay with us, used only to ship the box

Why small logos

A gift, not a uniform.

Clients keep gear they’d buy for themselves. A tonal logo on the chest or sleeve gets worn on weekends for years. A big one ends up in a drawer. We default to subtle, and you can always go bolder for your own team.

  • Tonal or one-colour, 3 to 3.5 in wide
  • Embroidery on caps and quarter-zips, always
  • We clean up your logo file for free

Questions

Good to know

What’s the minimum?

10 boxes, in steps of 5, up to 100 per order. Over 25 boxes the setup fee is waived and volume pricing kicks in.

How long does it take?

Mockups within 1 business day. Production starts once you approve and the sizes are in. Tell us the date you need them by and we’ll confirm it works before you pay.

Do I need everyone’s size and address?

No. Just a name and an email per client. They pick their own size and confirm their address from a private link.

What if someone doesn’t answer?

We remind them on day 2 and day 5. After 14 days we ship your default size to the address you gave us.

Can I see it before you make it?

Yes. You get photo-real mockups of every piece and the box lid. Nothing is printed until you approve.

Can you deliver all the boxes to my office instead?

Yes. Pick "Deliver to me" and we bring the boxes in bulk, which lowers the price.
